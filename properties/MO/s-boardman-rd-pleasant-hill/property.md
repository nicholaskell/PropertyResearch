## Land / Development Read
The central issue is **net upland yield**, not gross acreage. The $240k basis is only ~$4,860/ac, but a meaningful share is floodplain and the parcel is split by Boardman Road. Conversely, road frontage on both sides and the claimed sewer line could make the usable uplands disproportionately valuable.

The City of Pleasant Hill AG district, if applicable, is intended for 10+ acre agricultural/open-space tracts and lists a 10-acre minimum lot, 250-ft minimum width, 30-ft front/rear and 15-ft side setbacks. A denser development would therefore likely require rezoning/planned-development approval rather than a simple by-right split. City subdivision standards require new non-agricultural lots to connect to public wastewater unless specially approved, making the claimed sewer location a key diligence item.

### Development concepts to test
1. **Low-capital acreage split:** 3–4 premium upland acreage homesites using existing road frontage; preserve floodplain as recreational/open land.
2. **Rezoned sewer-served subdivision:** potentially higher value but engineering, floodplain, stormwater, sewer capacity and entitlement costs could be substantial.
3. **Private estate/homestead:** retain most acreage; place house/shop on upland; use bottom ground for pasture, crops, trails and recreation.

## RV / Build-While-Living
If Cass County zoning governs, County Code §400.262 allows one RV as temporary living quarters in A/R-R/R-S/R-1 while constructing or repairing a permanent dwelling. It requires the temporary-housing application with the dwelling permit, allows up to one year plus one extension tied to the dwelling-permit extension, permits temporary electric/public water, and requires approved wastewater or a portable waste tank. This is **not yet confirmed as the controlling rule for this parcel**; Pleasant Hill municipal jurisdiction must be established before relying on it.
