## Land / Development Model

### Zoning and subdivision rules
R-40: 1 dwelling per gross acre; 40,000-sf minimum lot; 50-ft minimum width. Standard R-40 setbacks are generally 70 ft on major streets, 50 ft on County-maintained streets, 25 ft on other streets, 10-ft side, 50-ft rear.

Jefferson County minor subdivision rules allow no more than four lots under 4 acres; divisions into lots of 4+ acres can also qualify. More than the minor-subdivision allowance requires preliminary plat review. Minor subdivisions using a private road generally require a 20-ft-wide road with 8 inches compacted rock; road grades cannot exceed 14%. County's current checklist states newly created onsite-septic/well lots need 2 acres.
