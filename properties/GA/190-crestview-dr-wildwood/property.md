## Development Read

The location is the compelling feature: Wildwood is immediately outside Chattanooga and the community has direct I-24/I-59 access. The parcel is priced at roughly $10.5k/gross acre using 14.22 acres, but gross acreage is not developable acreage.

Georgia onsite-sewage rules are a major gating item. For subdivisions relying on septic, written County Board of Health approval of water/sewage plans is required before development/sale, and predevelopment review can require boundary, topo and high-intensity soil information. Public water is required for a proposed subdivision when available within 1,000 feet under the state rule. This makes soils/perc and water-extension economics the first technical diligence items.

### Preliminary strategy scenarios

1. **Few larger homesites (capital-efficient):** likely best first concept until geometry/soils are known. Use existing frontage where possible, avoid an internal road, and sell 2-5 buildable acreage lots if legal frontage and perc support them.
2. **Smaller 1-2 acre lots:** potentially higher gross sellout but much more dependent on R-1 dimensional rules, frontage, septic spacing, water taps and subdivision standards. Do not underwrite until survey/soil/road rules are known.
3. **Build-to-sell:** Chattanooga proximity supports a deeper finished-home buyer pool than rural Dade County alone, but vertical construction materially raises capital at risk. One-at-a-time construction after entitlement is safer than simultaneous builds.
