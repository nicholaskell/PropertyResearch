## Access

## Zoning & Buildability
Listing identifies **Agricultural (A)** zoning. Boulder County's A district lists a **35-acre minimum lot size**. County FAQ says subdivision creating parcels under 35 acres is generally not allowed, with only limited exceptions. This 10.03-acre parcel therefore should be treated as a **one-homesite parcel, not a multi-lot development parcel**, unless Planning gives parcel-specific written confirmation of an unusual exemption.

Any building-permit development on a vacant unincorporated parcel requires **Site Plan Review (SPR)**. Obtain the seller's actual approved plans/docket number and confirm whether approval is current, transferable and consistent with the desired product.

## Temporary RV Occupancy
**FAIL for the user's construction-transition strategy.** Boulder County states that travel trailers, fifth wheels and RVs are not dwellings and may not be lived in permanently, during home construction, or on vacant property. Recreational camping is limited to **14 days per year**.

Insurance availability/cost needs direct quoting before acquisition.
