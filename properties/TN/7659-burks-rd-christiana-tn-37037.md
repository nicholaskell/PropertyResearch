## Zoning & Buildability

MLS calls the zoning “farm,” but Rutherford County should confirm the actual mapped district for parcel 154 01000 R0082773. The county's AR Agricultural Residential district is designed for crops, animal husbandry, dairying, forestry and similar rural activities, with a five-acre minimum. Single-family detached dwellings are permitted. Accessory buildings and home-based businesses are recognized subject to Chapter 11 rules. On lots over one acre, non-agricultural accessory structures may total up to 5% of lot area; agricultural buildings are exempt from that total. A private noncommercial garage/workshop may potentially be built before the house by Board of Zoning Appeals special exception.

A particularly important Thomas feature: on residentially zoned tracts of 15 acres or more, the ordinance allows one additional principal structure on a single lot if each structure can independently meet yard/area requirements. ADUs are also allowed subject to detailed rules. This creates a credible second-homesite path, but septic, water, access and exact zoning must be confirmed.

**Business warning:** Rutherford County's residential home-business rules are restrictive for Nicholas/Kell and Son. Noise, dust, odors, smoke or vibration may not be discernible at property lines; ordinary delivery vehicles are limited; minor businesses prohibit outdoor business-material storage; major home businesses require a BZA special exception. The land-use table does not permit Light Industrial in AR, and the use index classifies cabinet making and wood-products manufacturing/planing mills as Light Industrial. A true commercial sawmill/wood-products yard is therefore a significant zoning risk even though forestry and agricultural activity are favored. Firewood processing from on-site forestry may have a different classification, but it must be confirmed in writing with Planning before purchase.
