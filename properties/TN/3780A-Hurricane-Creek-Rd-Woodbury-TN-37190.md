## Zoning & Buildability

Cannon County's zoning resolution establishes A-1 Agricultural zoning. A-1 permits agricultural uses and single-family residential structures plus accessory structures without human habitation. Agriculture includes forestry, livestock and poultry husbandry. Other uses not specifically permitted are appeal uses.

Customary home occupations require a permit, are limited to household members plus one nonresident employee, must operate inside the dwelling/usual accessory buildings, and cannot create significant customer traffic or nuisance conditions. This is favorable for ordinary homestead/shop buildings but means commercial sawmilling/firewood/customer traffic should be specifically cleared with Cannon County Planning before purchase.
