## County Land-Use Rules Relevant to Development

Macon County's 2014 official zoning code states that A-1 is intended for agriculture/forestry and low-intensity/low-density development. Detached single-family dwellings are permitted. A-1 minimum area per dwelling is 1 acre with public water **and fire protection**, 2 acres with public water, and 3 acres without public water. Minimum width at the building setback is 100/150/200 feet respectively. A building lot must abut a publicly maintained road or permanent easement for at least 50 feet. These zoning minima do not override septic/soil, subdivision, fire-access, road, drainage or other requirements.

Recent Macon County Planning Commission agendas demonstrate that rural subdivisions are actively processed: examples include 15.53 acres into 7 lots, 18.80 acres into 9 lots, 12.87 acres into 5 lots and 20.26 acres into 16 lots. That proves subdivision is administratively possible in the county, **not** that this parcel can achieve comparable density.
