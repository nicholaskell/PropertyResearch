## Land / Build / Business Fit

AG zoning is structurally favorable. Montgomery County describes AG as protecting land suited to field crops, livestock and conventional agricultural activities. The tract is only ~6 acres and is reported sloped, so operational layout matters more than gross acreage. The property likely has enough room for a meaningful shop, greenhouse, chickens/small livestock and a compact firewood/lumber workflow if the usable bench areas are adequate.

The listing-claimed 4-bedroom perk site is especially valuable: it could support replacement/expanded residence or potentially a second homesite, but it does **not** by itself establish that a second dwelling or lot split is legal. Montgomery County subdivision rules require Planning Commission review when applicable and individual septic systems require TDEC approval. Verify whether the perk area is recorded/approved, its exact location, reserve area and whether it can serve an additional dwelling versus only replacement/expansion.

For Kell and Son/firewood/sawmill use, AG is promising but do not equate agricultural zoning with unrestricted commercial processing. County home-occupation rules are restrictive for ordinary residential home occupations, and AGC exists as a separate agricultural-commercial district. Get a written zoning determination for firewood processing, portable/permanent sawmill, log/lumber storage, equipment yard, customer pickup, signage and employees before treating commercial scale as cleared.
