## Zoning / Development / Business

Sumner County’s zoning framework allows home-based businesses and ADUs in residential contexts subject to detailed standards. Major home-based businesses require Board of Zoning Appeals special-exception approval; county rules can permit outdoor business-material storage with BZA approval. ADUs are permitted in residential districts with conditions, including owner occupancy, one ADU per lot, family/guest occupancy rather than rental, septic capacity and permitting.

**Important:** an ADU is not the same as creating an independently owned homesite for Thomas or another child. A future separate house/lot would require subdivision/zoning/frontage/septic feasibility and any recorded subdivision covenants to cooperate. On only 5.01 sloped acres, development optionality is therefore **LIMITED to PLAUSIBLE**, not assumed.

Exact zoning district and Langbrae Estates plat/covenants remain priority unresolved items. Do not commit to sawmilling, firewood retail, outdoor log storage, livestock, a large shop, or a second independent dwelling until those are checked.
