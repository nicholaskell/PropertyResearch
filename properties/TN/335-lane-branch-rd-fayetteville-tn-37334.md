## Zoning & Buildability

Lincoln County's A-1 Agriculture-Forestry-Rural Residential district permits agricultural/forestry uses, detached single/two-family dwellings, garages, barns, sheds, stables and farm buildings, forestry-related services, roadside sale of agriculture/forestry products, hunting leases, homemade furniture/crafts from a farm, and other agriculture-defined activities. A-1 minimum setbacks are 35 ft front, 25 ft rear, 20 ft side; separate lots/building sites generally cannot be under one acre.

**Important:** parcel-specific A-1 zoning was not independently confirmed. Also, the county's ordinary home-occupation rule is restrictive: it is conducted within the principal dwelling and nuisance/incompatible activities may be rejected. Commercial sawmilling/firewood processing that is not legitimately agricultural/forestry activity needs written zoning confirmation rather than assumption.
