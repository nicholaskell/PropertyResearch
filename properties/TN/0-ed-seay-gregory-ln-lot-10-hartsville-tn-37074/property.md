## Zoning & Buildability

The listing says **A1**. Hartsville/Trousdale County's zoning ordinance adopted March 23, 2026 labels the county agricultural district **TA-1 Agriculture-Forestry**. TA-1 permits accessory private garages, barns, stables, sheds and farm buildings, customary home occupations, and a small roadside stand for agricultural/forestry products produced on-site. Agricultural uses include poultry/livestock/horticulture/forestry subject to applicable laws.

This is generally favorable for a residence, shop/farm buildings, chickens and homestead uses. It is **not carte blanche for commercial sawmilling/firewood operations**. The ordinance allows major home occupations such as woodworking shops only through Board of Zoning Appeals approval and imposes residential-character/operational limits. Commercial-scale log storage, frequent customer traffic, heavy trucks or noisy processing therefore requires written zoning confirmation before being scored as clear.

## Temporary RV Occupancy

**Status: INVESTIGATE / likely restricted for the user's intended strategy.** The 2026 ordinance defines a travel trailer/RV as a temporary dwelling for travel, recreation and vacation and says it cannot be a permanent dwelling, principal structure or accessory structure. A separate temporary-dwelling provision allows a mobile home during construction, initially 12 months with extensions to a total of 18 months, but says this applies **in a residential district** and requires approved water/sewage. Because the subject is listed A1/TA-1, do not assume this pathway applies.
