## Zoning / Build Freedom

Cheatham County Commission minutes for 2024-03-18 identify **5360 Pond Creek Rd, Map 77 Parcel 16** and approve a request to change the zoning from **E-1 to R-1**. The same agenda/minutes state the parcel is in a **Special Flood Hazard Area**. Treat older listing/aggregator references to E-1 as stale unless the county later changed the zoning again.

Current county R-1 rules are designed for low-density detached residential use and expressly exclude commercial-character uses except qualifying special exceptions/home occupations. R-1 allows customary accessory structures, private residential garages, home occupations subject to Section 5.040, an accessory dwelling unit, and domesticated chickens subject to county standards.

The home-occupation section is restrictive for the intended business uses: no on-site retail sales, no outside storage of equipment/parts/materials used in the business, no commercial-vehicle storage, no traffic above residential norms, and no off-lot-detectable noise/vibration/fumes. County sawmill standards place **permanent sawmills in C-1/I-1/I-2/I-3** districts. This makes a meaningful on-site sawmill/firewood yard a poor fit without a rezoning/use determination.

**Accessory/ADU potential:** better than the business side. County rules allow one attached or detached ADU with the principal dwelling, subject to setbacks, owner occupancy, shared driveway/address/utility-meter rules and size/building-code limits. That is useful for a guest/family space, but it is not the same as an independent second lot/home.
