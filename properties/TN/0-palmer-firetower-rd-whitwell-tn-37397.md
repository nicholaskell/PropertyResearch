65-acre heavily wooded Cumberland Plateau tract on Palmer Firetower Road near Palmer/Whitwell. The property is exceptionally private and inexpensive in absolute terms for 65 acres, but it is not a conventional ready-to-build homestead: the listing explicitly markets it as completely off-grid and describes rolling topography, ridgelines and natural hollows with potential sites for a *small hunting cabin*. ## Zoning & Buildability

Marion County's official FAQ states **there is no zoning in unincorporated Marion County, Tennessee**. This is a major fit advantage for shops, equipment and flexible rural uses, but it is not a blanket exemption from rules.

County Building & Planning says residential structures and storage buildings/detached garages/metal buildings/pole barns **200 sq ft and larger require permits**, minimum setbacks apply, and structures use the 2018 IRC. County guidance also notes that multiple homes on one parcel should be planned with future subdivision requirements in mind.

This creates strong theoretical flexibility for Nicholas/Kell and Son/Thomas, subject to deed restrictions, access, septic and practical terrain.

## Animals & Homestead
