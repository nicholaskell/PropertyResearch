## Zoning / Build Freedom

Smith County Planning Office says it administers zoning, subdivision, GIS, building/electrical permits and floodplain rules outside municipal limits. The listing reports **Ag** zoning. Under the published A-1 Agriculture rules:

- farming and farm accessory structures are permitted;
- detached single-family dwellings are permitted;
- customary accessory buildings/structures are permitted;
- nurseries are permitted;
- dog/cat kennels and RV parks require special exception;
- minimum area per dwelling with public water is 1 acre;
- front setback 40 ft; rear 15 ft; side 25 ft with public water unless adequate fire protection changes it;
- maximum lot coverage is 50% of total/buildable area, whichever is less.

Agricultural uses receive broad protection in the ordinance. However, a commercial sawmill/firewood yard is not automatically a protected home occupation: home occupations must remain small scale and may not materially increase noise, dust, hazards or neighborhood impacts. **Commercial wood processing must be verified with the Land Use Administrator before relying on it.**
