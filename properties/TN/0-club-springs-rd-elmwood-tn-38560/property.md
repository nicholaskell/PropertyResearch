## Zoning / Buildability

Smith County's published zoning resolution establishes **A-1 Agriculture** as a low-density district intended for agriculture/forestry. Permitted uses include farming and farm accessory structures, detached single-family dwellings, manufactured/mobile homes on individual lots, customary accessory buildings, bed-and-breakfast/homestead uses, and nurseries. Dog/cat kennels are listed as a special exception.

A-1 minimum area per dwelling is 1 acre with public water and 3 acres without public water. Minimum building-line width is 150 feet with public water only and 175 feet without public water. Typical setbacks are 40 feet front, 15 feet rear, and 25 feet side where only public water/no qualifying fire protection is present. Maximum building coverage is 50% of the lot/buildable area.

Smith County's home-occupation rule is restrictive for noisy production: a home occupation must be conducted within the dwelling or approved accessory building, have adequate off-road customer parking, and must not increase noise, dust or health hazards. **Commercial sawmilling/firewood processing is therefore not automatically cleared merely because the parcel is agricultural.** Personal forestry/firewood and agricultural accessory activity are much easier fits; a business-scale sawmill/firewood yard needs a written interpretation from Smith County Planning/Zoning before purchase.

Greenhouse/nursery fit is notably strong: nurseries producing/cultivating flowers, shrubs and trees on-site for sale are expressly permitted in A-1.
