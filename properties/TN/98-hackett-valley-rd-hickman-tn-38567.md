## Zoning & Buildability

Listing reports agricultural zoning. Smith County A-1 regulations permit farming and related accessory structures, detached single-family dwellings, manufactured/mobile homes on individual lots, and customary accessory buildings. Minimum area per dwelling is 1 acre with public water and 3 acres without public water; setbacks vary with water/fire protection.

This is favorable for barns, shops and homestead structures. However, the county's home-occupation rule requires small scale and prohibits operations that increase noise, dust or health hazards. A serious commercial sawmill/firewood yard with customers, trucks and outdoor processing should **not** be assumed legal merely because the parcel is agricultural. Get a written zoning interpretation before purchase.

## Animals & Homestead
