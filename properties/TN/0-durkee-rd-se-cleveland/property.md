## Zoning / Development Rules

Bradley County's zoning resolution states FAR is intended for forestry, agriculture and residential development. Permitted uses include farming and farm accessory structures, detached single-family dwellings, mobile homes, customary home occupations, commercial nurseries, customary accessory structures, tent campgrounds and travel trailers. FAR minimum lot area is 7,500 sq ft with public water+sewer, 15,000 sq ft with public water, and 25,000 sq ft without public water/sewer, subject to larger TDEC requirements. Minimum width at building setback line is 75 ft with public water and 125 ft without public water/sewer. Typical FAR setbacks are 25 ft front, 10 ft side, 15 ft rear.

These zoning minima do **not** prove a high lot yield. Septic suitability, geometry, subdivision regulations, road standards and the existing access easement will control realistic yield.

Bradley County private-road regulations define private roads as access serving two or more owners. For six or fewer lots, a private road must meet county standards including a 20-ft compacted subgrade/gravel surface, drainage, max 15% grade, turnaround where applicable, and generally 40-ft ROW/easement if under 1,000 ft or 50 ft if over 1,000 ft. More than six lots triggers road/easement widths and construction requirements comparable to publicly maintained roads.
