## Zoning & Buildability
Cannon County A-1 Agricultural zoning permits agricultural uses and single-family residential structures/accessory structures. Minimum residential lot area is 30,000 sq ft with public water or 2 acres without public water, subject to health/septic requirements. Uses not specifically permitted require appeal. Cannon County considered/advanced restrictions on industrial uses in A-1 in 2026, so commercial sawmilling/firewood processing cannot be assumed permitted merely because zoning is agricultural. Small-scale agricultural/forestry activity is more defensible; business-scale operations need written Planning confirmation.
