# Research update — 2026-09-10

## Cannon County zoning / wood-business use

**Finding: PARTIALLY RESOLVED — material negative for commercial sawmilling.**

The current Cannon County Official Zoning Resolution, amended through 2026, states that the A-1 Agricultural District is intended for forestry, agriculture, agriculturally oriented uses and residential development. The only uses expressly permitted by right in A-1 are (1) Agricultural Uses and (2) single-family residential structures and non-habitable accessory structures. **All uses not specifically permitted require appeal.**

The same ordinance separately defines **“Lumber manufacturing (sawmill)”** as manufacturing/processing/sales involving milling forest products into rough or finished lumber. Lumber Manufacturing appears among the uses expressly permitted in the I-1 General Industrial District. This means a commercial sawmill should **not** be treated as an A-1 by-right use.

The county's April 2, 2026 Commission agenda also specifically included an amendment to prohibit industrial uses in A-1 Agricultural and R-1 Residential districts. The current amended ordinance is consistent with a restrictive reading: A-1 by-right uses are agriculture plus residential/accessory structures; unlisted uses require appeal.

### Implication for Nicholas / Kell & Son

- Normal forestry/timber management on the tract remains consistent with A-1's stated intent.
- A conventional **commercial sawmill/lumber-manufacturing operation is not a by-right A-1 use** and should be treated as a material business-use constraint.
- Firewood cutting for personal/agricultural/forestry use is different from a customer-facing processing/sales yard. The ordinance does not provide enough parcel-specific clarity to conclude that a recurring commercial firewood processing/sales operation is permitted by right.
- A customary home occupation is defined narrowly and is not a safe basis for assuming outdoor log storage, production noise, truck traffic or customer activity is allowed.

**Required external action:** obtain written interpretation from Cannon County Planning/Land Use Administrator for the exact proposed operation: portable/band sawmill, firewood processor/splitter, outdoor log/firewood/lumber storage, retail pickup, deliveries, employee/customer traffic, hours, and whether a special exception, rezoning or I-1 classification would be required.

**Status:** P1 business-use task = **PARTIALLY RESOLVED / BLOCKED for written parcel-specific interpretation**.

**RESCORE REQUIRED:** Yes. Existing Firewood & Lumber and Kell & Son scores were based on commercial-use legality being merely unresolved. The ordinance now provides affirmative evidence that lumber manufacturing/sawmilling is not an A-1 by-right use.
