## County Rules / Development Constraints

Cheatham County enforces zoning and subdivision regulations in unincorporated areas. The subdivision regulations require buildable lots, approved-street access, suitable geometry, drainage planning, and generally prohibit width:depth ratios over 1:4. TDEC requires a Subdivision Evaluation when land is divided into two or more lots for development using septic.

A December 2025 county zoning amendment requires **5-acre minimum lots in the Agricultural zoning district when served by public water**. This may or may not apply here because the listing's "REZ" field is not a verified county district.

County FAQ also states the first accessory structure may precede the principal building only on parcels of at least 15 acres in agricultural or residential districts. That is material to a shop/pole-barn-first plan on this 10-acre tract.
