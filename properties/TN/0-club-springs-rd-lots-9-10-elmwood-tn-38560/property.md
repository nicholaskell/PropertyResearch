## Zoning / Uses

Smith County Planning Office enforces county zoning, subdivision regulations, building permits and FEMA floodplain rules outside municipal limits. The listing calls this property `Agri-Res`; the current publicly posted Smith County zoning resolution defines A-1 Agriculture as low-density agriculture/forestry territory. Parcel-level official map confirmation is required before relying on A-1 rules.

If A-1 is confirmed, the ordinance permits farming and farm accessory structures, detached single-family dwellings, customary accessory buildings, bed-and-breakfast/homestead uses, and nurseries producing horticultural specialties. Minimum area per dwelling is 1 acre with public water, or 3 acres without public water; front setback 40 ft; rear 15 ft; side 15-25 ft depending on fire protection/public water; max lot coverage 50% of total/buildable area. Kennels are special exceptions and have stringent siting provisions, but ordinary ownership of three dogs is not the same as operating a kennel.

**Business warning:** county home-occupation rules say the operation is entirely within the principal dwelling or approved accessory building, household-run, with adequate parking and without increased noise, dust or health hazards. That makes a meaningful outdoor sawmill/firewood-processing business something to clear explicitly with Land Use Administrator before purchase. Personal wood processing and farm/forestry activity may be easier, but commercial intensity cannot be assumed.

Greenhouse/nursery fit is notably better: A-1 expressly permits nurseries, and farming/horticulture is consistent with district intent. On-site retail/farm-stand details should still be confirmed for the intended product mix.
