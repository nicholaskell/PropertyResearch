## Zoning / Subdivision

The Cheatham County zoning resolution uses A (Agriculture), E-1 and residential districts. In the A district, current bulk rules show a 5-acre minimum lot size with public water and a 125-foot minimum lot width at the setback line; without public water, the minimum remains 5 acres with 150-foot width. These rules are **not yet assigned to this parcel**, because the MLS field `Forrest` is not sufficient proof of the official zoning district.

Cheatham County classifies a subdivision involving a new/extended street, right-of-way or easement as a major subdivision. The subdivision regulations require legal public-way access or a compliant permanent easement; a permanent easement serving a separated tract is generally at least 50 feet wide, and access serving more than one lot can trigger public-way construction standards.

**Preliminary development implication:** If official zoning is A and public water is legally/serviceably available, 11.386 gross acres gives only a theoretical two-lot yield at the 5-acre minimum before subtracting constraints. The long access stem, hilly terrain, two creeks, frontage geometry, septic areas and stream/flood restrictions could reduce realistic yield to one additional homesite or make a two-lot split unattractive. This is not a high-density subdivision parcel under that zoning assumption.
