## Zoning & Buildability

The listing reports agricultural zoning. Cannon County's A-1 Agricultural District is intended for forestry, agriculture, agriculturally oriented uses and compatible residential development. Permitted uses include agricultural uses and single-family residential structures plus accessory structures without human habitation.

A-1 dimensional standards include a 30,000-square-foot minimum residential lot with public water and 2 acres without public water, subject to greater health/environment requirements. The county subdivision rules likewise state 30,000 square feet for public-water/private-septic residential lots or 2 acres with private water/private septic, subject to TDEC requirements.

**Important business limitation:** Cannon County's ordinance defines lumber manufacturing/sawmilling as an industrial use and explicitly lists lumber manufacturing as permitted in I-1 Industrial zoning. A-1 says uses not specifically permitted require appeal. Therefore, a commercial sawmill/firewood/lumber operation should **not** be assumed by-right here. A customary home occupation is limited: conducted by residents with no more than one non-resident employee, within the dwelling/usual residential accessory buildings, no outside stock display, no significant customer traffic, and no nuisance-type activity. This is a material issue for Kell and Son and Firewood/Lumber personas.
