## Zoning & Buildability

MLS reports AG zoning. Smith County's published A-1 Agricultural District permits farming and accessory structures, detached single-family dwellings, manufactured homes, public utility facilities and customary accessory buildings/structures. The district's stated intent specifically includes forestry and agriculturally oriented uses.

This is favorable for barns, shops tied to residential/agricultural use, animals and general homestead development. It does **not** automatically confirm a commercial sawmill, retail firewood yard, outdoor equipment/storage business, customer traffic or employees. Those activities need a written interpretation from the Smith County Planning Office before treating Kell and Son / Firewood-Lumber compatibility as clear.

## Animals & Homestead

A-1 is broadly favorable to normal agricultural uses. Chickens, goats, sheep, gardens, orchard and related accessory structures appear directionally compatible, subject to nuisance, sanitation and any property-specific restrictions. Smith County's A-1 text lists dog/cat kennels as a special-exception use, so ordinary ownership of three household dogs should be distinguished from a kennel operation. Confirm dog-count/kennel interpretation and fencing/setbacks.
