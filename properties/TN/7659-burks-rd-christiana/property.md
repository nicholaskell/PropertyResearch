## Build / Homestead / Business

Rutherford County allows accessory structures subject to permits/setbacks; agricultural structures may receive special treatment when tied to bona fide commercial agriculture. County guidance says fences generally require no permit. The zoning ordinance recognizes crop/animal raising and forest products as agriculture, but commercial processing is distinct; **do not assume a commercial sawmill/firewood yard is permitted by right**. Confirm the exact zoning district and proposed activity with Planning before treating Kell & Son/firewood use as viable.

The county states that lots 5+ acres may be created by deed with water access, at least 50 ft road frontage and soil sites; smaller divisions trigger plat review and additional requirements. This gives the tract theoretical development optionality, but steep terrain, road frontage geometry, septic sites and utility extensions will control the real number of lots.
