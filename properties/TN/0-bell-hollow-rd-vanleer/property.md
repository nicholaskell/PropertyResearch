## Zoning / Use Freedom

The listing labels zoning simply `AG`; do not assume whether the mapped district is A-1 or A-2 until the county zoning map/planning office confirms the parcel. Dickson County's zoning resolution shows that the A-2 Agricultural District has a 5-acre minimum lot size, one single-family dwelling per 5-acre lot, 150-foot minimum width at building setback, 75-foot front setback, and allows Type II home occupations subject to rules.

For Type II home occupations, county rules materially constrain a future wood/equipment business: an accessory business building may be up to 2,400 sq ft, minimum 5-acre lot, with substantial setbacks/screening; outdoor merchandise storage/display is generally prohibited; nuisance effects may not adversely affect neighbors; and trucks over 11,000 lb GVW are specifically implicated by the nuisance/delivery/storage language. Cabinetmaking/woodworking is listed among permitted Type II uses. **Commercial-scale firewood/sawmill/outdoor log storage should therefore be treated as VERIFY/possibly restricted, not assumed legal merely because the land is agricultural.**

Agricultural/homestead use appears promising, but chickens/livestock, greenhouse retail, accessory structures, second dwelling and subdivision should be confirmed against the exact mapped district and current zoning resolution.
