## Zoning / Build / Split Potential

MLS identifies **A-1 Agricultural** zoning. Cannon County's current zoning resolution states A-1 is intended for agriculture/forestry plus compatible residential development and permits agricultural uses and single-family residences/accessory structures. A-1 minimum lot area is 30,000 sq ft with public water or **2 acres without public water**, subject to TDEC requirements; typical non-farm setbacks are 30 ft front and 5 ft side/rear.

Cannon County subdivision regulations require, for residential lots with private water and private sewage, **2 acres minimum, 100 ft width at building setback, and 50 ft road frontage**, subject to TDEC. With public water/private septic the nominal minimum is 30,000 sq ft. Because this listing is already surveyed into three tracts and claims two septic soil areas, **development optionality is PLAUSIBLE-to-STRONG**, but the existing legal lot status, frontage of each tract and soil approval must be confirmed before valuing any split.

Commercial/industrial-style activity is not automatically safe in A-1. The county amended its rules in April 2026 to prohibit industrial uses in A-1/R-1. Firewood processing, sawmilling, customer traffic, outdoor storage and a boarding/greenhouse business need a written zoning determination rather than being assumed agricultural.
