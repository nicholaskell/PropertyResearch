## Development Analysis
Current county bulk standards show:
- Agricultural (A): 5-acre minimum with or without public water; 125-ft width with water / 150-ft without.
- E-1: 3 acres with public water, 5 acres without.
- R-1: 1 acre with public water, 5 acres without.
County subdivision rules also state subdivisions over two lots shall be served by a public water supply system. New/extended roads or certain utility/infrastructure work can trigger major-subdivision review.

This makes the exact zoning, public-water availability, parcel geometry, road status, TVA easement, and perc areas decisive.

### Plausible strategies
1. **Two-parcel resale / improve existing parcels:** Lowest entitlement risk and capital burden. Verify each existing parcel is independently legal/buildable; improve access/perc documentation and resell.

## RV / Initial Living
Cheatham County's current zoning resolution expressly allows a Temporary Use Permit for an RV during construction of a residence in residential, estate, or agricultural districts **after** a building permit for the permanent residence is issued. Water and sewage must be approved by the Health Department and/or utility system. Initial term is 9 months and renewals can extend total occupancy to 18 months.
