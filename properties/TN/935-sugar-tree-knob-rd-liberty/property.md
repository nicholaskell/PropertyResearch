## Zoning & Buildability
Nearby SMR lots on the same road are listed **AG**. Cannon County's A-1 Agricultural district permits agricultural uses and single-family residential structures/accessory structures; uses not specifically permitted are appeal uses. A-1 minimum residential lot area is 30,000 sq ft with public water and 2 acres without public water; general setbacks include 30 ft front and 5 ft side/rear for non-farm buildings. The subdivision regulations require 2 acres when both water and sewage are private, subject to TDEC soils/septic requirements.

**Important:** this is not yet official parcel-level zoning confirmation. Commercial sawmilling, retail/customer traffic, boarding and outdoor commercial operations should be treated as **VERIFY/BZA-risk**, not assumed legal. County home-occupation language is relatively narrow and centers on occupation within the dwelling.
