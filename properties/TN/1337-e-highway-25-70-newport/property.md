## Zoning / Development

Cocke County states that before development or use, a property generally needs a development permit, 911 address, TDEC septic permit when applicable, and zoning permit. The county currently states it does not require a building permit or certificate of occupancy for construction activities.

Cocke County subdivision regulations require generally **100 ft of frontage per lot** on a qualifying public/private road; special flag/cul-de-sac situations may use 50 ft, and sewer-served lots 75 ft. Exact frontage and parcel geometry are therefore critical before assigning a lot count.

**Important contradiction:** the listing repeatedly says "unrestricted," while MLS data reports R-1. Treat R-1 and county subdivision/zoning rules as controlling unless title and county staff establish otherwise.
