## Zoning / Homestead Potential

A secondary property-record source identifies zoning code **A**. Batavia Township's current A Agricultural district permits single-family dwellings, agriculture, animal/poultry husbandry, kennels and riding stables. For non-agricultural uses, the district generally requires 3-acre minimum lots and 200 feet width at the building setback line, with 50-foot front, 30-foot side and 60-foot rear setbacks. Agricultural buildings/uses on parcels 5+ acres receive substantial Ohio agricultural-zoning protection, subject to statutory exceptions.

If A zoning is confirmed, this is favorable for a personal homestead: chickens/livestock/ag buildings should be materially easier than in conventional residential zoning. A conventional residential dwelling must be at least 1,400 sq ft under the township resolution. Large shop/barn feasibility is promising but siting depends on buildable envelope, stream/floodplain, septic reserve and access.

**Important limitation:** A secondary dwelling unit is defined as attached/subordinate and family-occupied; this is not an obvious path to multiple independent micro-homestead dwellings on one undivided parcel.

## RV / Temporary Occupancy

Batavia Township Section 7.06 allows the zoning administrator to issue temporary-living-quarters approval up to 60 days for major calamity conditions; temporary living quarters beyond 60 days require Board of Zoning Appeals conditional-use approval. The resolution does not clearly establish an automatic right to occupy an RV throughout ordinary new-home construction. Treat construction-RV living as **UNKNOWN / written approval required** before purchase.
