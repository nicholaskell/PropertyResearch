## Governing Development Rules

Washington Township has **no zoning resolution or land-use plan**. Clermont County subdivision regulations therefore supply important dimensional standards for newly created residential lots in this unzoned area.

For single-family lots **without both public water and sewer**, county regulations require at least **2 acres / 87,120 sq ft** and **150 ft minimum width at the building line**. Because this site has water but no sewer, treat the 2-acre standard as the operative planning assumption unless Planning confirms otherwise. County unzoned-area setbacks are **50 ft front, 10 ft side, 35 ft rear**.

Any unsewered lot split must receive Public Health review showing adequate on-site wastewater capability. The county recognizes minor lot splits, five-acre-plus subdivisions, hybrids and major subdivisions. Existing private-street/common-drive standards and recorded Grants Run access rights may be the practical constraint on additional density.

Private streets in residential subdivisions generally require maintenance agreements/easements. County rules state a newly platted private street normally serves at least six dwellings; a vehicular travelway serving five or fewer lots is treated as a common driveway. The existing Grants Run plat predates the current proposal, so Planning/Engineer review is required before assuming additional lots can use the existing road.
