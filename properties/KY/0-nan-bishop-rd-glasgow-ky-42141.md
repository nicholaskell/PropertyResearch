# Property Summary

Large 132-acre rural tract west/southwest of Glasgow with an unusually low current asking price per acre (~$3,598/ac), mixed cleared and heavily wooded land, Buck Creek frontage, mature timber/habitat and strong hunting/recreation characteristics. Current MLS-fed sources show $475,000 after a July 8, 2026 reduction from the original $514,800. The tract has no on-site utilities listed and no dwelling.

The opportunity is potentially excellent for privacy, hunting, homestead expansion and wood-related uses, but it is not yet a strong contender because three permanent issues need verification before substantial weight should be placed on the acreage or price: address-level 300+ Mbps wired broadband, legal/practical heavy-equipment access over the reported easement/private-road approach, and buildability/flood/septic reality around Buck Creek.

This is a material due-diligence item for a residence, construction equipment, tractor/trailer operations, log trucks and future financing.

## Zoning & Buildability

Listing sources describe the parcel as Agriculture / residential land. Barren County's current code includes building/flood/subdivision rules, and the county building inspector states that strictly agricultural buildings on 10+ acres are exempt from building permitting (dwellings are not). The Joint City-County Planning Commission website currently advertises a **Proposed Barren County Zoning Ordinance - DRAFT**, so future regulatory changes are a live consideration.

Large acreage is favorable for shops/barns/agricultural structures, but a residence, commercial firewood/sawmill activity, outdoor storage, customer traffic, second dwelling and lot split should be confirmed with JCCPC before purchase.
