## Zoning & Buildability

Breckinridge County's official ordinances page states the county **does not have planning and zoning**; zoning applies in incorporated Cloverport, Hardinsburg and Irvington. Hudson is unincorporated. This is a major positive for accessory buildings, homestead flexibility and potentially home-business uses.

However, `no county zoning` does not mean `no rules`. Confirm with the county building inspector and health department before relying on:
- a house plus large shop/pole building;
- sawmilling/firewood processing and outdoor storage;
- customer traffic/signage/home business;
- additional dwelling or future lot division;
- setbacks/highway entrance;
- septic and potable water approval.

Breckinridge County Health Department performs onsite-sewage site evaluations, permits and inspections. Current published fees are $300 for site evaluation and $300 for installation permit. A conflicting MLS feed showed public sewer, while another exact-property feed shows sewer none; for underwriting, assume **septic must be proven** unless an actual public sewer tap is documented.
