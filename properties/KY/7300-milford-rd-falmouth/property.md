## Zoning / Use Findings

Pendleton County's June 2025 Rural (R) ordinance permits single/multifamily dwellings, crop/livestock farms, riding and boarding stables, hunting, regulated RVs, accessory garages/fences/storage sheds/greenhouses/gazebos, private stables/animals, home occupations, value-added agricultural production and agri-tourism. One ADU per parcel is allowed subject to water/septic, parking, size, location and owner-occupancy standards.

Important for Nicholas/Kell & Son: an **owner-operated specially skilled shop** is a conditional use in Rural zoning, with examples expressly including welders, mechanics, wood workers and **sawmills**. Home occupations conducted in an accessory building also require conditional-use approval. This is unusually promising for the intended workshop/sawmill use, but it is not by-right commercial freedom. Outdoor storage/noise/customer traffic should be discussed with Pendleton County Planning & Zoning before purchase.

Rural-zone minimum lot area is not fixed beyond ordinance/septic requirements. This creates theoretical land-division optionality, but road frontage, subdivision regulations, septic approval, parcel geometry and the pond/topography will control practical lot yield.

## RV / Temporary Living

Pendleton County Section 6.23 allows residence in an RV on a lot **without** an existing primary residence only with a zoning permit, on 5+ acres, for no more than six months in a calendar year, with approved water/sanitation arrangements, no more than a 50A pedestal, 50-foot setbacks, current registration and the RV remaining towable. On a lot **with** a primary residence/structure, the ordinance states residency in an RV may not exceed 30 days/year. Because this property already has finished living space classified as a residence, the practical plan is to live in the barndominium rather than rely on an RV.
