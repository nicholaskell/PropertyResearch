## Development Rules Confirmed

Edmonson County Land Development Regulations state:
- Major subdivision = **6 or more lots**.
- Minor subdivision = **5 lots or fewer**; a 3–5 lot subdivision with no new street construction/improvement and no major utility-line construction can proceed with final-plat approval only.
- Every lot must generally have **100 ft minimum public-road frontage** (50 ft on a permanent turnaround/curve).
- Where public sewer is unavailable, lots require County Health Officer approval for septic and cannot be smaller than **0.75 acre**; each septic system is individually permitted.
- Default residential setbacks where no zoning ordinance is in effect: 25 ft front, 25 ft rear, 15 ft side.

These rules make a **3–5 lot frontage-based minor subdivision** the first scenario to test. Six or more lots materially increases entitlement/infrastructure complexity.
