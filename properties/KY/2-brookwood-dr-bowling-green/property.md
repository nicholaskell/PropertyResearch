### Karst / sinkhole caution

Warren County sits in a karst landscape with extensive sinkholes, caves and underground drainage. Local planning maintains geophysical mapping specifically for contours, sinkhole/cave probability, wetlands, floodplain, septic suitability and karst risk. A conventional visual walk-through is not enough; build sites, septic and drainage need parcel-level review and potentially geotechnical input if suspicious depressions/features occur.
