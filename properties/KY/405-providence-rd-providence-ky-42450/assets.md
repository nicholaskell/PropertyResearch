## Evidence Rules

- Read relevant assets before resolving an Open Research Task.
- Prefer original/source documents over screenshots when both are available.
- Record provenance and date whenever practical.
- Do not alter source documents; add interpretation to `property.md` or a note file.
- Treat surveys, title commitments, deeds, disclosures and inspections according to what they actually establish; do not overstate them.
- Do not commit third-party listing/satellite imagery by default. Reference it unless the user intentionally supplies or archives a copy they are entitled to store.
- User-supplied due-diligence documents/photos may be stored here when the user wants them retained with the property.
