## Zoning / Buildability / RV

The MLS and multiple current listing sources identify the tract as **unzoned**. Bracken County nevertheless has a Planning Commission and countywide subdivision regulations. The regulations define land division and can apply to future splits even where conventional use zoning is absent.

No authoritative county rule was located that confirms full-time occupancy of a single RV/camper on a vacant parcel while building. The Bracken County Health Department does inspect septic systems and RV/mobile-home parks, but that does not establish legal single-site residential RV occupancy.

**RV verdict: UNKNOWN / INVESTIGATE.** Before purchase, obtain written confirmation from Bracken County / applicable building authority covering:
1. whether an RV can be occupied on this raw tract while a permanent residence is constructed;
2. whether an active building permit is required first;
3. maximum duration / renewal rules;
4. required septic or approved wastewater method;
5. water/power and 911-address requirements;
6. whether the bridge/driveway must be improved before permit issuance.
