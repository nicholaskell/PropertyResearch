## Zoning & Buildability

The listing places the property in Nahma Township. Delta County states that Nahma Township receives both building and zoning permitting through the county.

The exact zoning district needs parcel-map verification. If the parcel is **RR (Rural Residential)**, the county ordinance is unusually compatible with the intended lifestyle: single-family dwellings, agriculture/crop/pasture/orchards, managed timber, accessory garages/sheds/woodsheds, farm barns/equipment storage, roadside sale of property-grown produce, private pet kennels and home business are provided for. RR has a 5-acre standard minimum lot size, with 2-acre residential lots potentially available through conditional-use review if public-road frontage, services, soils/topography and other criteria are satisfied.

A standard Home Business is limited to an enclosed building, generally up to 1,000 sq ft, with only inhabitants plus one employee and restrictions on visible equipment/merchandise. More intensive business activity would need zoning review/possibly an expanded home-business conditional use. Sawmill/firewood commercial operation should **not** be assumed legal merely from the rural setting.
