## Zoning & Buildability

MLS labels zoning **Agricultural, Residential** and says splits are available. Escanaba Township's AP district permits agricultural production, crop cultivation, pastures, orchards, farmsteads and similar uses; feedlots, poultry farms, fur farms and commercial kennels are conditional uses with separation requirements.

Township Sec. 107 generally authorizes customary accessory structures with a permitted principal use and explicitly permits pole structures where covenants do not exclude them. This is favorable for additional barns/shops.

Important business caveat: Township Sec. 301's standard “home occupation” rules expressly apply to R-1, R-2, RR and LS/R, not AP, and prohibit outdoor storage/noise in those residential home-occupation districts. Because the intended firewood/sawmill/equipment business is more intensive than a normal home occupation, **obtain a written zoning determination for the actual parcel zoning and proposed business activities before purchase** rather than assuming agricultural zoning automatically permits commercial wood processing/customer traffic.
