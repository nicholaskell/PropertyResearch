## Zoning / Development
South Strabane R-1 and R-2 conventional single-family standards are materially similar for this concept: **0.5 acre minimum with public sewer / 1 acre without**, 100-ft width with sewer / 120-ft without, 40-ft front yard, 15-ft dwelling side yard, 40-ft principal rear yard, and 25% max lot coverage. R-2 also allows PRD as a conditional use. PRD density may reach 4 units/net acre after deducting slopes >=25%, with single-family PRD lots no smaller than 7,500 sf, but that is a more complex entitlement path and not the low-capital default.

The listing's three-parcel configuration and existing Clare Drive frontage are potentially excellent for a capital-efficient subdivision. The key unknown is how much of the 13.68 acres is actually on the buildable/utility-served side(s), and whether a new internal road is needed. Avoid assuming 20+ lots from gross acreage.

## Homestead / Lifestyle Constraints
This is fundamentally suburban-residential zoning, not permissive rural/agricultural zoning. R-2 expressly permits home gardening and domestic pets, but agriculture/livestock uses are not a normal permitted principal use. Large-equipment storage is also constrained in residential districts; commercial/construction vehicles over one-ton and tractors generally cannot be stored outdoors overnight except on active permitted construction sites or farms used for agriculture. This is a development play much more than a long-term unrestricted homestead/shop property.

## OGM / Rights Warning
