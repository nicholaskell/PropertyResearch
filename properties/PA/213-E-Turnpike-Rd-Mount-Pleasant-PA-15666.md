## Zoning & Buildability

The listing reports **Ag** zoning. Mount Pleasant Township's published zoning framework includes an A-1 Agricultural district intended for agriculture and low-density single-family residential development, and township rules allow agriculture/hobby-farm activity subject to setbacks. Accessory structures in A-1 can have favorable placement rules, and agricultural/agribusiness uses have dedicated provisions.

However, exact zoning must be confirmed against the official zoning map because the township itself identifies that map as the final authority. A second Thomas homesite cannot be assumed: subdivision/density, frontage, sewage and principal-dwelling rules need parcel-specific review.

## Animals & Homestead

Township rules define private stables/hobby farms in A-1 on at least three acres and regulate livestock/poultry shelter/manure setbacks. This amount of acreage appears promising for dogs, chickens and livestock if the parcel is confirmed A-1 and enough usable land exists.

Three household dogs do not appear to constitute a kennel merely because three dogs are present; kennel rules are aimed at breeding/boarding/sale/training and similar activities. Normal nuisance/control rules still apply.
