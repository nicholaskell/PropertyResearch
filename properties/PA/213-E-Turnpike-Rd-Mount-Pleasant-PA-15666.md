### 2016 deed: Mary I. Tepper Family Trust to Francis C. Tepper

Recorded Instrument **201605090014139**. This deed conveys the trust's undivided **2/3 interest** in the same 153.397-acre tract to Francis. It expressly states the intent that Francis obtain **all oil and gas royalties, rights, privileges, and full ownership of all subsurface rights** pertaining to the property. The deed explains that Francis already owned the remaining **1/3 of all rights, surface and subsurface**, so the transfer resulted in Francis holding **100% ownership of all rights, surface and subsurface**.

This is strong affirmative evidence of the Tepper chain's ownership of the O&G/subsurface estate, subject to existing recorded encumbrances.

### 2003 Kriebel Resources Oil and Gas Agreement

Recorded Instrument **200303310024429**, dated March 14, 2003. Landowners shown are **Mary Ida Tepper and John P. Sansone** and the gas company is **Kriebel Resources Company**. The agreement covers approximately **144 acres** and references Tax Map **53-5-62**, strongly tying it to the parent tract.

The agreement grants drilling/exploration/production rights and associated operational/surface rights. The recorded form contemplates wells, pipelines, roads/access, tanks, compressor-related equipment and other infrastructure associated with oil/gas operations. The primary term is **10 years**, reaching approximately March 2013, but continuation language tied to operations/production means it may have survived the primary term if held by production or qualifying operations.

The recorded agreement also states **SEE ADDITIONAL LEASE PROVISIONS — NOT TO BE RECORDED**. Those unrecorded provisions are not available in the uploaded recorded instrument and could contain additional surface protections or obligations; do not assume their contents.

### 2012/2013 royalty assignment

Recorded Instrument **201304090013601**, executed December 19, 2012 and recorded April 9, 2013. This document **does not assign Kriebel's operator/lessee interest to another gas company**. Instead, Mary Ida Tepper assigns her rights in the oil-and-gas agreement to the **Mary Ida Tepper Family Trust**, expressly including her **1/8 royalty interest** from native gas/oil sales and directing future note payments to the trust.

This document confirms the 2003 agreement remained relevant near the end of its primary term but does not establish whether the operator's lease remained active after March 2013.

### Coal and support rights

The 2016 deeds contain Pennsylvania statutory notices warning that the conveyance may not include coal/right of support and that the property may lack protection against mine subsidence. Those statutory notices alone do not prove a coal severance, but the deed also references prior **sale of coal and mining rights**, so coal/support rights must be traced separately before being treated as conveyed.
