## OGM / Mineral / Surface Rights

**Status: UNKNOWN — mandatory deed-chain research.**

Pennsylvania DCNR specifically states that oil and gas ownership is determined from deeds and that a thorough courthouse deed search is necessary. Westmoreland County's Recorder of Deeds provides searches by name, municipality, UPI/tax-map number, instrument number and legal criteria, and deeds are available back to the 1800s.

For this property, use APN/UPI **53-05-00-0-062** as the starting lead and trace the seller's deed backward until every oil/gas/mineral reservation, exception, lease, assignment or royalty interest is understood. Do not infer conveyance from the surface listing.

Required title/OGM questions:
1. Does seller own the oil, gas, coal and other mineral estate?
2. Do all of those rights convey?
3. Are royalties or executive/leasing rights severed?
4. Is the tract subject to any existing oil/gas lease, pooling or unitization?
5. Are there existing or reserved well pads, pipeline corridors, gathering lines or access roads?
6. Does any third party retain surface-entry or surface-use rights?
7. Are there coal-only or subsurface-only tax parcels associated with the tract?

Westmoreland County notes that tax-map numbers ending in certain 90-series identifiers can represent coal-only interests, reinforcing the need to check the complete parcel/title chain rather than only the surface parcel.
