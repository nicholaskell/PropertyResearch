# Property Summary

Large 126-acre timber/recreational tract on Golden Oaks Road in the New Freeport area of Greene County. The listing claims a hunting cabin with newer roof, maintained outbuildings/sheds, pasture/open areas, timbered hills, bottoms and a stream. At $365,000 the asking price is about $2,897/acre after reductions from $435,000. The permanent land package is attractive, but this is not yet a clean homestead/build candidate: exact wired broadband at the parcel, septic/perc feasibility, municipality/parcel boundaries, build/use rules, mineral rights, flood/wetland constraints and overlap with the Golden Oaks Road Natural Heritage Area all require confirmation.
