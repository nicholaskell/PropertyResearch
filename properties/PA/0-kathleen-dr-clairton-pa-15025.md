## Zoning and Subdivision

Current Jefferson Hills R1 dimensional rules are development-friendly on paper: **10,500 sf minimum lot size and 70 ft minimum width** for a single-family dwelling. The Borough subdivision ordinance defines a minor subdivision as no more than three single-family lots, all fronting an improved public street, with no new street/municipal-facility extension. Larger yield becomes a major subdivision with engineering, soils, erosion/stormwater, utilities and full preliminary/final plan review.

Subdivision design standards require lots to meet zoning, emergency accessibility, driveway grades no greater than 15%, stormwater controls and sanitary-sewer standards. New subdivision infrastructure can therefore become expensive quickly if an internal road, storm sewer or sewer main is needed.

R1 also allows farms/hobby farms and accessory agricultural uses, but the development persona is the better fit here. Chickens/ducks are permitted on residential parcels subject to acreage-based rules, but that is secondary to the subdivision thesis.
