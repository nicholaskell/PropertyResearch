Listing claims a 10-stall horse barn with public water and electric, silo, multiple pole barns/equipment sheds, partial fencing, creek, open pasture and woods. A 2022 perc test reportedly passed. Current ask is $499,000 after an original 2025 ask of $620,000; the property sold for $330,000 in November 2023.

## Zoning & Buildability

Washington Township's A-1 Rural Agricultural district permits agricultural use, animal husbandry, farms, single-family detached dwellings, multiple residential forms, home occupations, private/public stables and customary accessory structures. Agriculture expressly includes livestock, poultry, forestry and related farm structures.

Schedule II provides minimum lot area of 15,000 sq ft with 75 ft frontage where public water but no public sewer is available; A-1 yards are listed as 10 ft front, 5 ft side, 10 ft rear, with three-story maximum height and 25% maximum lot coverage. Creek-related structures have a 50-foot watercourse setback unless proper permits are obtained.

Important business limitation: ordinary home occupation rules prohibit outside storage/operations and use of accessory buildings. In A-1, contractor's yards, lumber yards, manufacturing, auto repair and broader business uses are listed as special exceptions. Therefore the property is excellent physically for Kell and Son/firewood/lumber but commercial-scale processing, customer traffic, outside log/firewood storage or a contractor yard should be pre-cleared with the zoning officer rather than assumed by-right.
