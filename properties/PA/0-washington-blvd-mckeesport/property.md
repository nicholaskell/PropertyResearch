## Land / Development

The parcel's biggest question is **usable acres, not gross acres**. Listing imagery shows a broad utility/power corridor with poles, brush and wooded slopes. The old marketing called the site suitable for single-family, multifamily, agricultural and agribusiness uses, but those are marketing claims, not entitlements.

Liberty Borough's subdivision ordinance requires Planning Commission and Council approval and can require roads, drainage, sewer/water and other improvements or financial guarantees. The ordinance generally defines division into 2+ lots as subdivision, with an agricultural exception for parcels over 10 acres not involving a new street/easement. A development plan therefore needs engineering-level access, grade, stormwater and utility review before assigning lot value.

**Development Optionality: PLAUSIBLE, confidence LOW-MEDIUM.** The three-parcel structure and metro location are positives; narrow frontage, terrain, utility easements and uncertain zoning/buildable bench area are negatives.
