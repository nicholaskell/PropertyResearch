# Property Summary

A very large, low-tax, wooded recreational tract on Redbank Creek with trail/internal-road infrastructure, hunting/fishing appeal, timber, and unusually good acreage-per-dollar for the region. The permanent negatives are equally important: the listing explicitly says **Surface Only**, terrain is described as rugged with hill climbs, the access road is dirt, utilities are only nearby, and parcel-level 300+ Mbps wired broadband has not been confirmed. This is much stronger as hunting/timber/recreation land than as a turnkey homesite, homestead, or operating business base.

## Zoning & Buildability

Madison Township has local building-permit/code administration and a sewage enforcement officer. Third-party zoning mapping reports “No Zoning,” which suggests relatively high land-use freedom, but this is not enough to declare the intended uses approved. County subdivision/land-development rules, sewage permitting, UCC/building permits, driveway/road occupancy, floodplain rules and environmental permits can still govern development.

Before purchase, obtain written confirmation for: residence, large accessory shop, outdoor equipment storage, woodworking, firewood processing/sales, sawmilling, livestock/poultry, future second dwelling/lot split, and any commercial/home-occupation use.
