## Development Read

This parcel has unusually useful development ingredients for its size: reported **547 ft public-road frontage**, roughly **10.5 acres**, available public water plus electric/gas, and a township A-1 framework that allows detached single-family dwellings on **1-acre minimum lots with 120-ft minimum width**. The township's subdivision checklist classifies **over 10 lots as major**, so a 3-5 lot frontage-oriented concept should remain in minor-subdivision territory if otherwise compliant.

The preliminary constraint is frontage. Five 120-ft-wide lots would nominally require ~600 ft if every lot independently consumes minimum frontage, exceeding the reported 547 ft. Four frontage lots require ~480 ft and therefore fit arithmetically before survey/road-access/septic constraints. A fifth lot may be possible only through geometry/access arrangements that satisfy the ordinance/SALDO and marketability; do not count it yet.
