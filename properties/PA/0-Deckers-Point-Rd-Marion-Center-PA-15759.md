# Property Summary

Large 123-acre vacant-land opportunity in East Mahoning Township, immediately east of Marion Center. Asking price is $338,250 ($2,750/acre). The tract is described as mostly reclaimed land with open fields, heavy cover/new timber growth, mature trees, a small pond and established gas-well/access roads. It lies on both sides of Deckers Point Road and has approximately 3,482 feet of frontage. The acreage/price combination is compelling, but this is not a clean blank-slate tract: historical/current listings explicitly identify several gas wells and a pump house, making mineral/surface rights, well status, access agreements and setback/operational impacts major due-diligence items.

## Zoning & Buildability

East Mahoning Township is reported as having **no zoning**, which is a major positive for accessory structures and flexible rural use. This does not mean unregulated: Indiana County administers building/UCC requirements in East Mahoning, and the county SALDO can apply to subdivisions and land development. On-lot sewage permitting, floodplain rules, driveway/highway occupancy requirements and state environmental rules remain applicable.

A house, shop and homestead appear plausible, but the exact homesite is **not yet confirmed**. Obtain perc/septic feasibility, water/well expectations, setbacks from gas wells/facilities, and confirmation from Indiana County Planning that the intended house + large shop + outdoor storage/firewood/sawmill configuration does not trigger problematic land-development review.
