## Development Read

The property has two unusually useful development ingredients for a rural tract: reported frontage/access on two roads and reported public water/sewer availability. However, steep sections, Clean & Green rollback exposure, uncertain utility connection economics, and severed minerals prevent assuming a high lot count.

The township subdivision ordinance requires residential lots to abut by their full frontage on a publicly dedicated street. That strongly favors a **road-frontage lot strategy** over constructing a costly internal subdivision road. A township pre-application meeting with the engineer is required/recommended to classify a proposed subdivision.

### Preliminary scenarios

1. **Low-capital acreage split:** 3–5 road-frontage lots, roughly 5–12 acres each depending on geometry/topography, preserving a larger residual tract. Best initial concept.
2. **Mid-density frontage development:** approximately 5–8 lots if usable frontage, zoning dimensions, sewer/water taps and topography cooperate. Needs survey/topo and utility confirmation.
3. **Dense/vertical buildout:** not recommended at this stage. Infrastructure, grading, absorption and capital exposure could erase the cheap land basis.

Clean & Green is important: Pennsylvania generally allows qualifying separations of 10+ acre tracts to remain enrolled, while residential split-offs are tightly limited; incompatible changes can trigger up to seven years of rollback taxes plus interest. A conventional 5–8-lot project should be modeled assuming some rollback-tax exposure until the Washington County assessor confirms otherwise.

## Temporary RV / Camper

Current township code is unfavorable to the planned raw-land RV phase. §410-93 states that travel trailers, mobile homes, motor homes, campers and similar recreational vehicles may not be occupied or used as a dwelling except in an approved recreational vehicle park or for temporary visitors to a residential lot, limited to three months in a calendar year. On the present evidence, assume **you cannot legally live in an RV on this vacant parcel while building** unless the Township gives a written interpretation/approval.
