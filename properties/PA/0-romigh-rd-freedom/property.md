## Zoning & Development
New Sewickley's current zoning ordinance identifies **RA Residential Agricultural** and **MDR Mixed Density Residential** districts. In either district, a single-family dwelling requires **45,000 sq ft (~1.03 ac) minimum lot area, 150 ft minimum lot width, 40 ft front, 20 ft side and 40 ft rear setbacks**. In RA, agricultural operations require 10 acres; the district permits single-family dwellings, agricultural operations, commercial stables and forestry, among other uses.

The zoning minimum is **not** the practical lot yield. The parcel's apparent ~117 ft road neck is narrower than the ordinary 150 ft single-family lot width, so multiple independent frontage lots are not a realistic base assumption. Any meaningful subdivision likely needs an engineered common/private access concept, flag-lot treatment if allowed, or a new internal street meeting SALDO/fire/stormwater requirements.
