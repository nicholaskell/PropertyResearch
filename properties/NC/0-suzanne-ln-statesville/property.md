## Temporary RV Occupancy

**CONDITIONAL PASS.** Current LDC §2.20.2 permits a private RV campsite in RA. R34 allows one RV per parcel (up to three only with at least ten acres per RV), requires principal-structure setbacks and an approved sewer/septic system, and caps occupancy at 90 days per calendar year. R23 separately permits an RV as a temporary dwelling during construction after a residential building permit is issued and remains active, with approved wastewater. This does not authorize permanent RV residence.
