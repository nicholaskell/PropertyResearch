## Zoning / Use Notes

Levy County's current code says RR districts are intended for low-density rural residential use, generally without central water/sewer. The current use table permits bona fide agriculture and single-family dwellings in RR. RR minimum lot size is 3 acres, minimum width 150 ft, depth 200 ft, setbacks generally 30 ft front / 10 ft side / 30 ft rear, and 10+ acre RR lots have 10% maximum impervious coverage. Accessory dwelling units are possible under county rules subject to owner occupancy, wastewater capacity and district standards.

Home-based businesses are allowed under Florida/Levy rules only if residential character is maintained and nuisance impacts such as noise, vibration, smoke and dust are avoided; heavy equipment must be screened. This makes a true noisy sawmill/firewood yard uncertain even if a quiet home occupation is allowed. HOA covenants may be stricter than county zoning.
