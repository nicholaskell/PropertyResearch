## Zoning / Subdivision Framework

Harrison County's zoning ordinance (amended 2025-09-15) states major subdivisions are permitted in all zones except A-R; minor subdivisions are permitted in all zones and a parcel may be subdivided more than once through the minor-subdivision process. The older subdivision-control ordinance defines a minor subdivision as no more than three lots fronting an existing improved county-maintained right-of-way without new streets/municipal-facility extensions/public improvements. New lots must meet dimensional, access, health and drainage standards.

Table 1b requires, for A-R, 1-acre minimum lots and 150 ft frontage; R-1 without approved sewage collection requires 1 acre and 120 ft frontage. **Actual zoning for this parcel has not yet been positively confirmed**, and the county itself recommends parcel-specific zoning confirmation before land-use decisions.

The subdivision ordinance also states land unsuitable because of flooding, drainage, slopes ≥15%, rock formations or topography shall not be subdivided/developed unless adequate approved mitigation is provided. That provision is central here.
