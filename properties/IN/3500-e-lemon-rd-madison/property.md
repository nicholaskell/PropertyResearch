## Zoning / Building / RV

Jefferson County's UDO permits accessory structures in all districts subject to standards. Structures over 200 sq ft such as pole barns, garages, greenhouses and stables require an Improvement Location Permit. In AG, accessory structures are exempt from the residential-district limits that otherwise cap quantity/area and can precede the primary structure. Exact parcel zoning must be confirmed in the official GIS before treating AG rules as applicable.

County building-permit guidance requires a septic permit or Health Department approval letter for projects involving plumbing when not on municipal sewer. For a future permanent house, treat septic feasibility as a gate item.

The RV rule is unfavorable to the user's landing strategy: permanent RV residential occupancy is prohibited outside an approved campground/RV park. Recreational use outside one is limited to 14 consecutive days, no permanent utilities, and highway-ready configuration. A manufactured-home construction accommodation may exist elsewhere in the UDO, but it is not an RV exception.
