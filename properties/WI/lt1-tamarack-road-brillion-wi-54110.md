### Managed Forest Law

The listing says the property is enrolled in MFL. A purchaser generally inherits the MFL order and management plan unless land is withdrawn. Wisconsin DNR says MFL enrollment lasts 25 or 50 years and ownership transfers require the new owner to accept program obligations or pursue withdrawal. Building rules depend on enrollment date. For 2017-or-later entries, buildings/improvements generally are not permitted on MFL land unless exclusively for storage; older entries have different residential-development tests. DNR provides a construction-site withdrawal process, generally 1-5 whole acres, but zoning minimum acreage can affect the amount and remaining land must continue to qualify. Withdrawal can trigger a tax plus a $300 fee. The exact MFL order number, enrollment year, open/closed status, management plan, scheduled harvests and withdrawal estimate are critical due-diligence items.
