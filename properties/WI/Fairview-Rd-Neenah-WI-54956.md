# Property Summary

28.25-acre mostly open agricultural parcel in the Town of Clayton at the northeast corner of Fairview Rd and Oakwood Ave, immediately south of US-10 and adjoining the Friendship Trail. Asking $699,900 ($24,775/acre). The parcel is unusually well positioned for Fox Cities growth and development optionality, but the asking price embeds a substantial development-location premium and is difficult to justify purely as a private homestead.

## Access

## Zoning & Buildability

Assessment/zoning sources identify **A-2 General Agriculture**. Town code describes A-2 as accommodating large/small farms and hobby farms, with scattered residential lots allowed, adjacent to urbanizing areas. Agriculture/forestry and related uses have favorable treatment, but a specific dwelling, accessory-shop plan, land division, commercial firewood/sawmill use and customer activity should be confirmed against the current 2026 code and site-plan rules.

This location is in an active growth corridor. Town planning documents call for road/infrastructure improvements around Hwy 10/76 and Fairview, and recent 2025-26 proceedings show major residential/mixed-use rezoning and subdivision activity nearby. That is positive for appreciation/development but negative for permanent rural isolation.

## Animals & Homestead

A-2 is fundamentally compatible with hobby-farm/agricultural character. Chickens/livestock and outbuildings should be materially easier than suburban zoning, subject to current animal/setback rules. Three large dogs appear feasible with ample containment area; verify any current dog-count/kennel provisions. Open acreage makes fencing straightforward but expensive.

## Rights & Restrictions

OGM/mineral severance is not a prominent regional risk comparable with Appalachian candidates. Nevertheless, deed/title should confirm mineral, utility, drainage, trail, highway, access and other easements. The Friendship Trail adjoins the north side, so trail-related easements/buffers should be confirmed.

## Taxes & Carrying Cost

Listing tax: **$131/year**. 2025 assessor roll shows only ~$6,500 assessed value because almost all land is in agricultural-use classifications. This is **not** a realistic post-build/post-conversion tax estimate. Converting acreage to residential/development use can materially increase assessment and may create agricultural-use conversion/rollback consequences. Model taxes from the proposed end use before purchase.
