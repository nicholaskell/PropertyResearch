# Property Summary

## Zoning & Buildability

The listing labels zoning **AG**. The property appears to be in the Town of Grant, which states that it is under Shawano County zoning jurisdiction. County working-land districts permit agricultural accessory uses, farm animals and agricultural structures; the exact mapped district (likely FP/FH/OAR rather than a generic MLS 'AG') must be confirmed.

County code can accommodate home occupations and agricultural home occupations, but commercial-scale sawmilling/firewood/customer traffic may require conditional-use analysis depending on the exact district and operational intensity. Do not assume a full commercial yard is automatically permitted.

The listing's second 'buildable site' is a major asset but needs written county/town confirmation of dwelling entitlement, parcel status, septic permit/condition, setbacks and whether a separate residence can actually be built/occupied.
