## Zoning & Buildability

The property is in the **Town of Center**, where Outagamie County zoning applies. The listing identifies dual **Agricultural / Residential** zoning. County AGD zoning permits agriculture and single-family detached dwellings; county rules also recognize accessory uses/structures customarily incidental to principal uses.

The exact zoning boundary matters. The property should not be treated as a single unrestricted 4-acre agricultural parcel.

Key open questions:
- Where exactly does the residential/agricultural zoning line fall relative to the barn, garages and open land?
- Can another large shop/pole building be added, and on which portion?
