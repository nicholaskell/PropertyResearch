## Zoning & Buildability

Property records identify the parcel as residential in the Village of Ashwaubenon. Village guidance states that on lots over 25,000 sq ft, total accessory-building area is generally limited to **936 sq ft** under the ordinary residential accessory-building rules; typical maximum accessory-building height is 15 ft. Rural Estate parcels have different rules, but this property has not been confirmed as Rural Estate.

This is a major baseline weakness for Nicholas/Kell and Son: a meaningful modern shop, sawmill/firewood yard, large equipment building or truck-oriented operation is difficult to fit within ordinary suburban-residential rules and neighborhood compatibility.
