Confirm with the Town Zoning Administrator whether the following are permitted, conditional, or require rezoning/site-plan approval: large accessory shop before/after a dwelling, outdoor equipment/trailer storage, customer traffic, commercial firewood processing, sawmill operation, lumber/log storage, vehicle/equipment repair, employees and signage.

## Animals & Homestead

Town zoning is favorable for this acreage. Household livestock requires at least 2.5 acres, fencing, and animal numbers are subject to county conservation rules. A-2 is specifically agricultural/hobby-farm oriented.

Town zoning materials state there is no chicken-number limit on A-2 parcels and no chicken enclosure requirement specific to A-2, although registration/other animal-health rules may apply. This is strongly favorable for chickens, goats, sheep and a flexible homestead.

For dogs, Town Ordinance 2020-007 generally limits a household to **3 dogs over five months** without a kennel license. That matches the current three-dog household exactly. Town zoning defines a private kennel differently in A-2, but the separate dog ordinance should be treated as the controlling practical count until the Town confirms otherwise.
