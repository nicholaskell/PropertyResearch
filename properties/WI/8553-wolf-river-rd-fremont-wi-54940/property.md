## Zoning / Business / Homestead

Winnebago County’s current zoning data identifies the Town of Wolf River R-1 district as **Single Family - Low Density Residential**. The Town comprehensive-plan material lists one-family dwellings and noncommercial gardens as permitted R-1 uses, contrasting with agricultural districts that expressly list farming, livestock, greenhouses and nurseries. This makes the existing farm use important but does **not** establish that new commercial or agricultural expansion is unrestricted.

For the intended firewood/lumber operation, the critical question is whether the property has lawful nonconforming agricultural/accessory rights, whether the larger parcel carries a different district, and whether a home occupation/conditional use can cover equipment, outdoor storage, processing, customer pickup and noise. **Do not buy this property assuming a commercial sawmill or visible firewood yard is legal.** Get a written zoning determination covering both parcel IDs.

For homesteading, the existing horse/farm/chicken infrastructure is a positive practical signal. Backyard chickens and household livestock exist as defined accessory-use categories in Winnebago County’s zoning framework, but the applicable Town of Wolf River rules, setbacks and animal-count requirements need confirmation for this exact zoning configuration.
