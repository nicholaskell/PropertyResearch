## Zoning & Buildability

The parcel is reported as **AGD General Agricultural District** under Outagamie County zoning. County code allows single-family dwellings and agricultural uses and provides for accessory uses/structures. AGD also permits a home-based business subject to limits: incidental to residence, no more than two non-family employees, residential appearance, traffic consistent with an agricultural area, no off-lot detectable noise/vibration/glare/fumes/odors/electrical interference, and no more than 30% of a principal/accessory structure used by the business.

Sawmills are listed as a special-exception use via the agricultural district provisions; AGD special-exception uses generally cannot be within 500 feet of another existing residence or a recorded subdivision boundary. This 500-foot rule could materially affect a commercial sawmill/firewood plan and must be mapped before relying on approval. Town permit materials expressly contemplate detached garages/sheds and storage buildings, supporting meaningful future outbuilding potential subject to county zoning/setbacks.
