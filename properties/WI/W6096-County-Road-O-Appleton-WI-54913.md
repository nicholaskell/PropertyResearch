## Zoning & Buildability

The parcel is reported as **AGD General Agricultural District** under Outagamie County zoning. The current county code allows agricultural and residential uses and accessory structures. A home-based business is possible but subject to operational limits.

A sawmill is a special-exception use through the agricultural-district provisions. AGD special-exception uses imported from the AED district generally cannot be located within **500 feet of another existing residential dwelling other than the owner's or within 500 feet of the exterior boundary of a recorded subdivision plat**. This is a material constraint for a commercial sawmill and must be mapped against the actual parcel and surrounding residences before relying on approval.

Meaningful accessory-building expansion appears plausible, but exact setbacks, dimensional rules, driveway permits and any shoreland/floodplain overlays still need parcel-specific confirmation.

## Animals & Homestead

The listing explicitly advertises equestrian use and includes an 8-stall barn, paddock and fenced pastures. That is exceptional existing infrastructure for a flexible homestead.

For the three dogs, current Outagamie guidance indicates that an owner of three or more dogs in county-zoned municipalities may need to contact the Zoning Administrator for a special-exception permit when not operating a state-defined kennel. The existing dog grooming/boarding building is encouraging, but current legal/permit status and transferability must be confirmed.
