## Zoning & Buildability

MLS-fed sources describe the subject generically as **Residential**, which is not enough to determine the governing Oconto County district. The property is in the **Town of Pensaukee** and Oconto County zoning applies. A nearby 20.15-acre parcel at 4252 Deprey Rd is recorded as Agricultural zoning, and county permit archives show substantial accessory-building activity on Deprey Rd and elsewhere in Pensaukee. That makes a flexible rural district plausible, but it is not proof of the subject's zoning.

The current Oconto County zoning ordinance, revised 2026-04-23, is favorable in rural districts:

- **Rural Residential (RR)** permits agricultural production, crops, livestock, forestry, single-family residences, animal shelters/barns/sheds and accessory structures; the ordinance describes RR as a mix of farming, forestry and non-farm residential uses.
- **Agricultural (A)** permits normal agricultural/forestry uses and under the county ordinance allows smaller-scale firewood processing; portable sawmill and larger firewood-processing uses can require conditional approval.
- **Forest (F)** is also comparatively compatible with forestry/portable sawmill/firewood uses, subject to ordinance thresholds and conditional-use provisions.
- A conventional **R-1** classification would be materially less flexible for the intended business/homestead uses.

Therefore the exact district is a **gating due-diligence item**. Before purchase, obtain written confirmation from Oconto County Zoning of:
- exact district for parcel 034302001324A;
- maximum/accessory building rules and setbacks;
- home occupation/property-services use;
- outdoor equipment, trailer, log, lumber and firewood storage;
- woodworking and vehicle/project work;
- firewood processing volume/sales;
- portable or permanent sawmill rules;
- customer traffic, signage and employee rules; and
- path to a second dwelling/lot split/ADU or family homesite for Thomas.
