## Zoning & Buildability

The listing reports **Agricultural** zoning and the property is in the Town of Rockland under Manitowoc County general zoning. The exact district for each subject PIN remains unverified. Exclusive Agriculture (EA) exists in Rockland and is designed to preserve agricultural land; permitted residential and accessory uses differ materially from Small Estate or General Agriculture districts.

A listing statement that a field is a potential building site is not a zoning determination. Before purchase, obtain the PIN-level zoning map and written county confirmation covering:

- primary residence/cabin;
- large detached shop/garage and agricultural buildings;
- chickens, goats, sheep and other livestock;
- outdoor equipment/vehicle storage;
- home occupation, woodworking, firewood processing and sales;
- sawmilling/lumber storage/drying;
- future second dwelling, ADU, family residence or land division for Thomas.

If the tract is EA, Thomas's independent future homesite and nonfarm commercial activity deserve especially careful review rather than being assumed allowed.
