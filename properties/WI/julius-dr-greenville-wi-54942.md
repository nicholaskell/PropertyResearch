## Zoning & Buildability

Current listing zoning is Agricultural. Greenville's AGD dimensional table generally shows a 5-acre minimum gross lot area, 200-foot lot width and 160-foot frontage (120 feet on cul-de-sacs). New farm dwelling units have separate dimensional provisions. Greenville's subdivision ordinance also regulates lot creation, and larger residential development may require rezoning, platting, road construction, stormwater and utility extensions.

The strategic signal is important: Greenville's 2040 plan anticipates continued substantial residential growth, and current planning activity around School Road/Julius Drive shows active subdivision/development pressure in this corridor. Do not equate that with an automatic entitlement for this assemblage.
