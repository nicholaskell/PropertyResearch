## Zoning & Buildability

The property is in the Town of Hancock, Delaware County. Town code is unusually favorable for the intended rural use profile:

- One- and two-family dwellings, ordinary residential accessory structures and home occupations are exempt from site-plan review.
- Agriculture and timbering activities are exempt from site-plan review.
- New residential lots generally require 1 acre, 150 feet frontage, 25-foot side/rear setbacks and 35 feet from road centerline under the site-plan standards; subdivision guidance calls for 150 feet frontage and adequate septic area.
- Accessory structures generally must be in side/rear yards and at least 10 feet from property lines; retired storage trailers/containers may not be used as accessory structures.
- Building permits are required for most meaningful structures; small one-story sheds up to 144 sqft can be exempt from a building permit.

This is a strong regulatory starting point for shops/outbuildings. Commercial-scale firewood/sawmill/customer activity beyond a customary home occupation should receive a written jurisdictional determination from the Town before purchase.
