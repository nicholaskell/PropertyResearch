## Zoning & Buildability

Town of Erin zoning code states AR (Agricultural-Residential) covers all Town areas not otherwise designated. Park Hill Rd is not among the named AR-1/AR1-R/R-1/R-2 corridors in the code, so AR is the working classification pending parcel-level confirmation.

AR permits farm uses, dairy/farm stock, riding horses, domestic fowl, forestry, greenhouse/nursery/truck garden, and one- or two-family dwellings per tax parcel. Permitted accessory uses include barns/silos/customary agricultural structures, storage/tool/garden structures, seasonal farm stands, farm ponds and wildlife habitat. Agricultural structures must generally be at least 100 ft from property lines.

Home occupations are permitted but must remain subordinate, generate no unusual traffic and produce no offensive noise/smoke/odor/heat/glare/electronic disturbance beyond the property. This is a meaningful caution for commercial sawmilling/firewood processing. Agricultural/forestry activity may provide a different legal pathway, but business use should be confirmed in writing with the Town before purchase.
