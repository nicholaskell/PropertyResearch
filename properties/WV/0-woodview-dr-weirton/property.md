## Planning / Development Rules
The City of Weirton Unified Development Ordinance governs zoning and subdivision. A **minor subdivision** is no more than 3 lots, each with required frontage on an existing street, with no new street, municipal-facility extension, or new public improvements. Four or more lots, a new street, municipal-facility extension, or public improvements make it a **major subdivision**.

If parcel zoning is R-1, the current UDO development table lists single-family minimums of 6,000 sq ft lot area and 60 ft lot width, with 20 ft front, 30 ft rear and 5 ft side setbacks. Those dimensional minimums do **not** establish practical yield: terrain, road/frontage, utilities, stormwater, geotechnical conditions, neighborhood pattern and market absorption will dominate.

The UDO says an RV is not intended as a permanent dwelling. Temporary construction-related contractor trailers may exist during active construction/sales, but **sleeping and cooking accommodations are prohibited**. Until the Planning Department gives a written interpretation, assume living in an RV on this raw parcel during construction is not allowed.
