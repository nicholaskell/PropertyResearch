# Property Search Criteria

## Goal

Find rural/semi-rural U.S. properties that provide the best long-term combination of usable land, buildability, broadband, privacy, move-in readiness, carrying cost, market access and value.

Always distinguish **Confirmed / Listing Claim / Estimate / Unknown**. Never assume unknowns are favorable.

## Nicholas priorities

- 300+ Mbps wired broadband; fiber preferred.
- Useful/operational acreage over nominal acreage.
- Shop/garage space or a practical/legal place to build it.
- Freedom to add shops, garages, pole buildings, barns, sheds, greenhouses, coops, pavilions and related improvements.
- Privacy, room for 3 large dogs, homesteading and animals.
- Woodworking, tractors/equipment, firewood processing, sawmilling/lumber and small-scale farm activity.
- Reasonable taxes/carrying costs.
- Access to viable markets for firewood, lumber, woodworking and farm goods.

## Brittany priorities

A move-in-ready house. Cosmetic work is acceptable. Immediate major structural, foundation, roof, water, HVAC, electrical/plumbing, septic/well, kitchen/bath or gut-renovation work is a major negative.

Key question: **Can we comfortably move in and improve it over time?**

For vacant land, evaluate homesite practicality, utilities, access, septic/well feasibility and build complexity instead.

## Fatal-flaw screen

Before scoring, classify each as **CLEAR / INVESTIGATE / FAIL**:

- Wired broadband
- Legal/practical access
- Buildability/zoning
- Severe wetlands/flood/topography/soil constraints
- HOA/deed/private restrictions
- House habitability or homesite feasibility
- OGM/mineral/surface-right conflicts where relevant
- Unreasonable carrying costs
- Any property-specific hard constraint

A FAIL can override a high numeric score.

## Required research

Research beyond the listing using current listing/MLS-fed sources and authoritative public sources where available.

Evaluate:

- Current listing/offer status, including active-offer/contingent/pending status and whether another offer can be submitted.
- Asking/original price, reductions, DOM, sales/listing history, assessment and taxes.
- Broadband: FCC + ISP availability, speed/technology, fiber at house/road/nearby, funded expansion and extension potential.
- Land: parcel configuration, total/constrained/usable/prime usable acres, topography, wetlands, floodplain, soils, drainage, bedrock, streams, setbacks and septic suitability.
- Access: frontage, public/private/deeded access, maintenance agreements, driveway and equipment/construction access.
- Zoning/buildability: shops, barns, pole buildings, sheds, coops, greenhouses, additions, home business, outdoor storage, firewood processing and sawmilling.
- Animals: dogs/kennel rules, chickens/roosters, goats, sheep, horses/livestock.
- Rights/restrictions: HOA, covenants, easements, ROW, private roads and, where relevant, oil/gas/mineral/coal/timber/surface rights, leases, wells and pipelines.
- Taxes: current taxes, reassessment risk, special assessments and agricultural/forest/current-use programs.
- House condition: roof, foundation, water, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, well/septic, windows, kitchen/baths and deferred maintenance.
- Utilities: well/septic, gas/propane, electric service, 3-phase potential and utility easements.
- Location: always include distance/drive time to **Green Bay, Wisconsin**.
- Market access: identify the best realistic 30/60/90/120-minute markets for firewood, lumber, woodworking and farm goods.
- Nuisances: highways, industry, CAFOs, rail, airports, landfill, quarry/mine, oil/gas, pipelines, transmission lines, ranges and other material concerns.
- Value/comps: fair value, offer range and effective cost-to-goal.

## Land model

Do not equate acreage with value.

**Total Acres → Constrained Acres → Usable Acres → Prime/Operational Acres**

Prime/operational acres are land especially useful for buildings, equipment, gardens, animals, wood processing, recreation or other intended uses.

## Cost-to-goal

Classify missing features:

**Existing / Easy Value-Add / Major Value-Add / Difficult / Impossible**

Always distinguish **“doesn't have it” from “can't have it.”**

**Effective Property Cost = Purchase Price + Required Improvements + Desired Improvements**

Permanent characteristics receive the most weight: usable land, location, access, zoning, broadband, wetlands/topography, privacy, rights/restrictions, taxes and market access.

### Overall opportunity

Use roughly **55% Nicholas / 45% Brittany**, then apply judgment for price/value and hard constraints.

The central question is:

**At this price, how good is this opportunity considering what exists, what is missing, what can change and what it costs to get there?**
