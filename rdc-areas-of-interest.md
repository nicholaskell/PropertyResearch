# RDC Areas of Interest

This is the working geographic shortlist for **Rural Dating Catchment (RDC)** research and property discovery. It is intentionally separate from individual property records.

These are **areas to investigate, not predetermined winners**. Rankings should change as RDC counts, land inventory, pricing, regulations, broadband, development economics, traffic, and growth-direction evidence improve.

## Primary discovery standard

Prefer corridors that meet all three:

- **Suburban Pool Depth:** LARGE or better
- **Rural Interface:** EXCELLENT or better
- **Date Friction:** LOW or LOW-MODERATE

Use **RDC-45** as the primary dating-catchment comparison, with RDC-30 and RDC-60 showing near and extended access.

The practical acquisition screen is approximately **$250,000 maximum** and normally **10+ acres**, while allowing smaller parcels when frontage, subdivision yield, location, or development economics are exceptional.

## Control

**Green Bay / Appleton, Wisconsin** remains the control/baseline when useful. Compare candidate RDCs against the user's current northeastern Wisconsin access rather than only against one another.

## Next quantitative pass

For each corridor:

1. Select representative rural-interface origin points rather than metro centroids.
2. Build/approximate 30-, 45-, and 60-minute road catchments.
3. Aggregate ACS women age 30–39 and marital-status categories inside the practical suburban catchment.
4. Preserve margins of error; use rounded ranges when appropriate.
5. Separate never-married from divorced/separated where feasible.
6. Estimate pool concentration/density and date friction.
7. Map population/housing growth direction and metro-connected commuting patterns.
8. Measure current acreage inventory and price per usable/developable acre.
9. Test typical subdivision rules, infrastructure burden, broadband, and path-of-development evidence.
10. Compare **RDC quality × acreage/$ × developability × growth direction × broadband × regulatory freedom**.

## Discovery workflow

```text
Metro candidate
    ↓
RDC corridor screen
    ↓
RDC-30 / RDC-45 / RDC-60 + friction + growth direction
    ↓
Current land inventory / price screen
    ↓
High-performing RDC × Development zones
    ↓
Individual parcel Investigator
    ↓
Subdivision / entitlement / finance / exit analysis
```

Do not search entire states or broad MSAs by default once a qualifying RDC corridor has been identified. Concentrate discovery on the rural interface and expand outward only when inventory or economics justify it.
