# Brittany

## Core Question
**Can we comfortably move in and improve the property over time without immediately taking on a major renovation?**

## Priorities
| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Move-In Readiness | 30 |
| House / Major Systems | 20 |
| Kitchen / Baths | 15 |
| Layout / Functional Space | 15 |
| Immediate Work Required | 10 |
| Overall Condition / Appeal | 10 |

## Preferences
- Paint, flooring, fixtures, decorating and normal cosmetic projects are acceptable.
- Major structural, foundation, water, roof, HVAC, electrical/plumbing, septic/well, kitchen/bath or gut-renovation work is a substantial negative.
- For vacant land, substitute homesite quality, utilities, septic/well feasibility, road/driveway and construction complexity.
