# Dogs — Three GSD/Husky Mixes

Score the property for safe, practical long-term life with three large, active dogs.

## Hard Concerns
- Local rules/HOA/deed restrictions must allow three large dogs without an impractical kennel requirement.
- Avoid properties where safe outdoor containment is effectively impossible.

## Evaluation Notes
- GSD/Husky mixes are large, active and potentially capable jumpers/diggers; evaluate realistic secure-fence options rather than assuming an ordinary low fence is sufficient.
- Favor usable open/wooded areas for exercise, exploration and supervised off-leash activity.
- Penalize immediate frontage on high-speed roads, especially when the house or primary yard is close to traffic.
- Favor separation from neighbors to reduce barking/noise conflict and provide safer outdoor use.
- Check dog-count, kennel, nuisance and fencing rules plus HOA/deed restrictions.
- Consider terrain hazards, water/ice, livestock conflicts, nearby loose dogs/wildlife and other property-specific risks.
- Bonus for practical mudroom/garage entry, durable dog-access areas and easy creation of a large fenced yard or run.
