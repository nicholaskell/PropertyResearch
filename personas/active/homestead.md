# Homestead

Evaluate the property for a flexible household homestead rather than maximum commercial agricultural production.

## Priorities
| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Usable Agricultural / Garden Land | 18 |
| Animal Freedom | 15 |
| Water / Soils / Growing Potential | 12 |
| Outbuilding Freedom | 12 |
| Privacy / Rural Compatibility | 10 |
| Garden / Orchard Potential | 8 |
| Utility / Infrastructure Practicality | 8 |
| Food / Farm Market Access | 7 |
| Taxes / Agricultural Programs | 5 |
| Expansion / Flexibility | 5 |

## Evaluation Notes
- Consider chickens, coops, goats, sheep and other reasonable small livestock plus applicable acreage/setback/fencing rules.
- Evaluate garden/orchard locations for sun, drainage, soils, water access and wildlife pressure.
- Favor practical locations for coops, barns, shelters, greenhouses, raised beds, compost, feed storage and equipment.
- Consider pond potential/existing water features when useful, while distinguishing beneficial water from wetlands/flood constraints.
- Evaluate agricultural/current-use tax programs and potential rollback obligations.
- Market access should consider farm stands, farmers markets, local-food customers and population/income within practical driving range.
