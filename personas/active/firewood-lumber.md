# Firewood and Lumber

Evaluate the property specifically for firewood processing, sawmilling, lumber production/storage and related wood-product opportunities.

## Priorities
| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Legal / Zoning Compatibility | 20 |
| Operational Yard / Shop Space | 15 |
| Customer Market Access | 15 |
| Log / Material Supply Access | 12 |
| Truck / Trailer Access | 10 |
| Neighbor / Noise / Dust Separation | 10 |
| Covered Lumber / Firewood Storage | 6 |
| Power / Utilities | 5 |
| Drying / Solar / Airflow Site Quality | 4 |
| Taxes / Carrying Cost | 3 |

## Evaluation Notes
- Determine whether sawmilling, firewood processing, outdoor storage and product sales are permitted or realistically approvable.
- Favor flat, well-drained operational areas with room for logs, rounds, splits, lumber stacks, kilns/drying, equipment and safe workflow separation.
- Evaluate log-truck, dump-trailer and customer access plus loading/turnaround space.
- Consider nearby timber resource, tree-service/arborist activity, logging, sawmills and other potential raw-material sources.
- Market access should evaluate realistic firewood and lumber demand within 30/60/90/120 minutes, not merely distance to the nearest town.
- Penalize close neighbors or restrictions likely to conflict with saw, splitter, tractor, loader, truck, dust or outdoor-storage activity.
