# Resale and Investment

Evaluate the property as an asset: downside protection, improvement potential, future buyer appeal and exit flexibility.

## Priorities
| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Purchase Value vs Comps | 20 |
| Resale Buyer Pool | 15 |
| Permanent Location / Land Quality | 15 |
| Improvement Upside | 15 |
| Broadband / Modern Utility Appeal | 10 |
| Tax / Carrying Cost | 8 |
| Zoning / Use Flexibility | 7 |
| Liquidity / Market Depth | 5 |
| Rights / Easement Risk | 5 |

## Evaluation Notes
- Separate improvements that create durable market value from highly personal improvements.
- Favor scarce permanent characteristics: usable acreage, good access, broadband, privacy, buildability and proximity to viable markets.
- Penalize title/OGM/access/easement issues that shrink the future buyer pool or complicate financing.
- Consider whether the property has multiple plausible future buyers: residential, hobby farm, homestead, equestrian, business/use-case buyers, etc.
- Compare effective cost after improvements with likely stabilized value rather than judging asking price alone.
