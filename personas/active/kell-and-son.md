# Kell and Son Limited Co.

Evaluate the property as a base for property/land work, equipment, firewood, lumber/sawmill and related future business activities.

## Hard Concerns
- Business/home-occupation rules must not clearly prohibit the intended activity.
- Practical truck, trailer, tractor and equipment access is required.
- Neighbor proximity/noise restrictions must not make normal operations inherently conflict-prone.

## Evaluation Notes
- Check home-business, commercial activity, outdoor storage, equipment, signage, customer traffic and employee restrictions.
- Specifically evaluate firewood processing/storage, sawmilling, lumber drying/storage, woodworking and equipment/vehicle work.
- Favor room for a shop, covered storage, log/firewood yard, trailers, tractors and maneuvering/loading areas.
- Consider semi/log-truck access, driveway geometry, bridges/culverts, road weight limits and year-round access.
- Evaluate single/three-phase power availability and the practicality of supporting welders, compressors, woodworking and sawmill equipment.
- Favor separation from neighbors and layouts that direct noise/dust/traffic away from the residence.
- Market access should consider likely customers for firewood, lumber, woodworking, property services and related goods.
