## Future Homesite
- Strongly favor properties where Thomas could legally build his own home later if he chooses.
- Research whether an additional dwelling, lot split, land division, family dwelling, ADU or other practical path exists.
- Evaluate a realistic future building location for usable acreage, setbacks, road/frontage requirements, driveway access, septic/well feasibility, utilities and broadband extension.
- Prefer a homesite that can provide meaningful independence/privacy while remaining part of the larger property.
- Flag zoning, minimum-lot-size, density, deed, access or septic constraints that could make a second homesite difficult or impossible.

## Trucks / Projects
- Thomas is interested in Ford trucks and should have somewhere practical to work on, park and store trucks/project vehicles.
- Favor an existing garage/shop or a legal, practical location for a future truck-oriented shop.
- Consider door/ceiling height, slab/floor, electrical service, heat, lighting, tool space and vehicle maneuvering when information is available.
- Evaluate outdoor parking/storage rules and practical room for multiple vehicles, trailers, parts and projects without creating neighbor or zoning conflicts.
- Bonus for properties where a future homesite and truck shop could form a semi-independent area.

## Marketplace / Market Access
- Facebook Marketplace and similar private-party markets matter for buying/selling trucks, parts, tools and projects.
- Use nearby population centers and 30/60/90-minute reach as a proxy for Marketplace inventory and buyer pool.
- Favor reasonable access to auto parts, salvage yards, hardware/tool stores, fuel and vehicle services.

## General Notes
- Strong broadband matters for education, work, gaming/entertainment and social connectivity.
- Rural property should not create unreasonable isolation from employment, college/training, shopping or social opportunities.
- Favor a bedroom/personal area with reasonable acoustic and visual privacy while he lives in the main house.
- Do not assume Thomas wants to remain in the main household long-term; score both present comfort and a credible path to future independence on the property.
