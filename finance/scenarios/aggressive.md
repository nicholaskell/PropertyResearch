# Aggressive Capital Scenario

Use the optimistic home-sale proceeds from `finance/buyer-finance.md`.

Goals:
- Show the upper-end purchase/improvement flexibility if sale proceeds are strong.
- Preserve at least the minimum cash reserve unless the user explicitly chooses otherwise.
- Allow more capital to be deployed toward down payment or transformational improvements.
- Do not assume future refinancing, appreciation or equity borrowing will rescue an otherwise weak purchase.
