# Balanced Capital Scenario

Use the expected home-sale proceeds from `finance/buyer-finance.md`.

Goals:
- Preserve the minimum cash reserve.
- Use enough down payment to keep the payment within the target range when practical.
- Retain improvement capital when it materially improves the property or avoids expensive financing later.
- Compare extra down payment versus retaining cash for missing high-value features.
- This is the default scenario for ranking serious contenders unless the user specifies otherwise.
