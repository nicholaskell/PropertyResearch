# Conservative Capital Scenario

Use the conservative home-sale proceeds from `finance/buyer-finance.md`.

Goals:
- Preserve the full minimum cash reserve.
- Avoid relying on optimistic sale proceeds, uncertain rebates, future refinancing or future equity borrowing.
- Prefer purchase structures that remain comfortable if improvement costs run high.
- Show whether the property still works when assumptions are intentionally cautious.
