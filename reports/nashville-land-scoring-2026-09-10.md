# Nashville Land Scoring — 2026-09-10

First-pass comparative scoring of the 32 retained Nashville-area land listings after applying the 5-acre minimum. Scores are deliberately **provisional / Low confidence** because all imported parcels still have unresolved P0 fatal-flaw categories. Unknowns were not treated as favorable.

## Scoring method

Scores use the current active repository criteria and personas. They emphasize permanent characteristics already exposed by Zillow discovery—price, acreage, Nashville-market proximity, likely privacy/operational separation, and broad metro-market access—while applying a material uncertainty penalty for unresolved broadband, access, zoning/buildability, land constraints, deed/restriction/rights, taxes and true usable acreage. These scores are for portfolio triage, not final purchase decisions.
